## Counter-evidence
- 29.5 days is the average time from new moon to new moon (the synodic month). Single months vary by several hours around it. 'About' covers this. The page also gives 27.3 days for one orbit, which careful viewers may confuse with it, but the line correctly uses the new-moon-to-new-moon figure.

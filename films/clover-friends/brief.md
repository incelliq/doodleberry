# Brief — "Clover Friends"

Second film on the engine. Same science as `films/clover` (legume nitrogen fixation
in crop rotation), retold as a character story with a "with vs without" comparison,
using the techniques studied in two reference reels of this genre (devices and craft
only; no characters, layouts, scripts or music copied).

| field      | value | why |
|------------|-------|-----|
| mode       | launch-story / explainer hybrid | characters carry the setup and payoff, an explainer character carries the mechanism |
| subject    | why farmers rotate clover | one cause and effect, told as before/after |
| viewer     | feed scrollers, kids, gardeners | they care about the characters first, the science second |
| end state  | know: clover first, then corn — the clover feeds the soil for the next crop | said, shown as a side-by-side result, and titled |
| length     | ~50 s, 9:16 | dialogue-led story needs more room than a 30 s explainer |
| frame rate | 30 | character animation |
| cast       | Mina (human, young farmer), Wiggs (earthworm explainer), Kernel (hungry corn seedling), Clover (sprout), narrator (time card), rhizobia (silent) | original designs |
| voices     | one Piper voice + WORLD re-voicing per character; lip-sync from each character's own audio | distinct voices was the strongest device in the references |
| devices    | lip-sync, eyelid acting, blurred foreground layer, rain and wet ground, time-skip card, side-by-side result, soil-crumb pops, underground dive, nodule window | everything the engine now supports |
| facts      | facts.json; conversational lines without claims are marked `fact: null` | nothing invented |
| autonomy   | unattended; gates logged in ledger.md | user away |

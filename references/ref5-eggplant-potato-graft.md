## What it teaches
1. **Hook with an action, not a sentence.** The knife cut lands before the first word (1.2 s). The title adds nothing, and it still got 12M views.
2. **The misconception is voiced by a character** ("we look different, so we can't be related"), then disproved by a visible result. That's the Veritasium misconception-first idea done as comedy.
3. **A two-harvest reveal across the soil line:** above-ground and below-ground payoffs in the same frame. Our cutaway engine is built for this.
4. **Time card as a beat:** the skip itself is a joke (deep voice, full frame, ~2 s).
5. **Background gags in the soil** (a skeleton and a bomb) add texture without costing narration time.
6. **Short explainer:** 15 s, three claims, an aphorism to close. Shorter than refs 2 and 4.
7. **Mix and pace:** LRA 3.5 LU (ours 1.1–1.5); ≈184 wpm while speaking.

## Fact check (for our own use)
The premise is real horticulture: eggplant (*Solanum melongena*) grafted onto potato (*Solanum tuberosum*) rootstock is a known
dual-crop graft (sold in the UK as an "egg and chips" plant). "Cousins" is fair (same genus). "Nutrient pathways connect" is a
simplification of vascular union across the graft.

# Brief — Handshake (interaction and locomotion demo)

Engine demo for WP-11. Pip and Tam walk towards each other along curved paths (Catmull-Rom curves, a little
perspective as they come forward), their feet planted while the root moves, each walk ending on a passing step. They
meet, look each other in the eye and shake hands: Tam holds out a hand to the meeting point and pumps it, Pip takes
Tam's hand wherever it is (an IK target on another character), both blended in and out by IK/FK weights.

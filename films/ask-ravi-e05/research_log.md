## Counter-evidence
- The page puts the streak down to 'glowing hot air'. A fuller account is that the light comes from air and vaporised meteoroid material heated and ionised along the path. Ravi's line follows NASA's wording exactly, which is acceptable. Most shower meteoroids are dust or sand-grain sized rather than 'rocks', and the page notes they range 'from dust particle to boulder size'.

# Brief — Night planes (compositor demo)

Engine demo for WP-36: a night shot built from depth planes. Far to near: a starry sky with a glowing moon, hills,
a band of mist (masked and screen-blended), the stage where Ravi stands, and grass in the foreground. A slow camera
push shows the parallax (far planes move less), depth of field softens the planes away from focus.

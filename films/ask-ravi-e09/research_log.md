## Counter-evidence
- During a total lunar eclipse the Moon usually turns reddish rather than simply dark, and the same page says so. 'Gets darker' is accurate but incomplete, which is fine for this line.
- A solar eclipse is seen only where the Moon's shadow falls, which the page notes. The line's 'the Moon's shadow falls on Earth' is accurate.

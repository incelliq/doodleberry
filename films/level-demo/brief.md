# Brief — Campfire (drawn levels demo)

Engine demo for WP-35: hand-drawn animation as drawn levels, playing beside a rigged puppet. A campfire (12 drawings
on twos, looping) burns while a doodle walks across on a 12-drawing walk cycle on twos, and Ravi, a rigged character,
invites them over. The drawings are stand-ins made by `tools/make_level_demo.py` with MyPaint brush strokes until an
animator draws them on the Studio drawing page.

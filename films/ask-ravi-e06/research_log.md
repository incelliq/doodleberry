- f2: (Non-blocking) 'It goes round in about ninety minutes' joins two page sentences: the ISS is in LEO, and one LEO orbit takes about 90 minutes. This is a valid inference. Suggested: None needed.

## Counter-evidence
- The ISS's orbital period is about 92 to 93 minutes, so 'about ninety' is fine.
- The page describes momentum and gravity as forces that are 'balanced'. A physicist would object, because momentum is not a force. Ravi's line avoids that wording and uses only the 'always falling but moving sideways fast enough to miss' picture, which is correct.

## Research leads
- Average CHIRPS rainfall over Sri Lanka (or the Mahaweli and Kelani basins) for Oct-Dec and Jan-Mar, 1981 to now, and split by ONI El Niño years.
- Re-check De Silva & Hornberger's finding: during El Niño, rainfall rises in October-December and falls in the following January-March.
- Read Zubair & Ropelewski (2006, J. Climate 19:1567-1575) on the strengthening ENSO-northeast monsoon link; the abstract was not reachable.
- Add the Indian Ocean Dipole as a second driver, and check the November 2026 CPC update (due 12 November 2026).

## Cautions
- Not a weather forecast: speak in tendencies and probabilities, not 'there will be floods' or 'drought'.
- Floods and droughts hurt people; avoid real disaster footage and keep advice to official sources.
- El Niño is one driver among several; past patterns may not repeat.

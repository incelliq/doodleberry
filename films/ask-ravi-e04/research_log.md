## Counter-evidence
- 27 million °F is about 15 million °C. For audiences outside the US, consider a Celsius caption or line ('about fifteen million degrees Celsius'), but it would need its own sourced fact.
- Strictly, hydrogen nuclei (protons) fuse in the core, not whole atoms. The page says 'atoms', and Ravi's 'the hydrogen smashes together' is an acceptable simplification for children.

# Moon Night (plan 2.14 test production)

An original four-part story with the generated cast, made once and cut many ways: a full 16:9 film and four 9:16
episodes with continuous music, cut on bars.
1. Momo can't sleep: the moon is too bright. 2. At the library Ravi lends Momo a telescope.
3. On the street Priya joins. 4. On the hill at night they look at the moon together.

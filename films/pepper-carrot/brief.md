# Brief — Potion Ready (development showcase)

A 10-second scene that shows what the platform does with richly drawn, rigged characters imported from Blender
cut-out rigs: painted raster parts, smooth skinned knees and skirt, spring hair and tail, sprite lip-sync from a voice,
blinks, gaze, brows and hand poses.

- **Cast:** Pepper and Carrot, rigs by Nikolai Mamashev after David Revoy's Pepper&Carrot (CC-BY 4.0). Built locally
  with `python -m dbe puppet.from_blend '{"recipe": "cast/recipes/pepper.json"}'` (and `carrot.json`); the rigs and
  the built puppets are not in git (see assets/manifest.json "external").
- **Status:** internal development showcase, not for publishing as Doodleberry's own characters. Credits must carry
  the attribution line from each recipe.

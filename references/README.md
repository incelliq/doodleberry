# Reference studies

Notes and measurements from short-form farm-science cartoon reels studied for craft.
Only devices and measurements are recorded here: no footage, frames, scripts, characters
or music from the references are stored or reused. Films in this repo use original casts,
scripts, layouts and scores.

## Ref 1 and ref 2 in brief
- **Ref 1:** side-view soil cutaway in the lower half; plants and props with eyes; translucent underground callout; small arrows; one-word captions centred low; a two-character dialogue scene to close. Drawing: heavy blurred background trees (depth of field), soft clouds, a hedge band, cel-shaded leaves with glints, heavier silhouette lines than interior lines, fine branching rootlets, a light topsoil band with clods and mounds over dark subsoil, yellow shock marks.
- **Ref 2:** setup comedy between two prop characters (0–14 s) → full-frame "Later…" card read by a deep announcer (1 s) → consequence shown by set dressing: rain, wet dark soil, puddles, a rot stain (16–24 s) → static two-shot of an expert explaining (25–60 s). Four distinct voices (≈420/270 Hz characters, ≈98 Hz announcer, ≈110 Hz explainer). Out-of-focus foreground leaves along the bottom edge. Eyelid acting (smug half-lids, content closed eyes). No hold longer than 0.47 s.

## What it teaches
1. **Title formula:** a relatable, slightly embarrassing body question in plain words, framed in the third person ("Why does she…").
   It got 8.4M views, 14× the bent-tree reel and the most of the seven.
2. **Inside-the-body staging:** a flat, symmetric anatomical cross-section with organs as characters. It's cheap to draw
   in our 2D engine (a cutaway like the clover soil plane) and maps onto the 3D x-ray idea.
3. **The argument is the explanation:** two organs blaming each other lays out the two parts of the mechanism
   before the doctor names it.
4. **No time card, one cut:** health topics move straight from the inside view to the clinic.

## Fact check (for our own use)
Stress urinary incontinence (leakage with coughing, laughing or exercise because the urethral closure can't resist the
rise in abdominal pressure) and urethral bulking injections are both real. The reel omits that first-line treatment is
usually pelvic floor muscle training, with bulking agents, slings and other options after that. A film of ours on this
would need a urology or NIH/NIDDK source and should mention pelvic floor training.

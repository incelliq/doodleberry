# Brief — Stretch (squash, stretch and constraints demo)

Engine demo for WP-11 part 2 (puppet format v0.9). Tam's balloon floats off along a curve (a prop following a path);
Tam's arm stretches past its reach to catch the string (stretchy IK, per-part stretch), the hand switches to its
holding drawing because the forearm points up (drawing substitution by angle), the other arm copies the reach
(copy rotation), the head squashes on the catch, and the face moves with the words (whole-face lip-sync). Tam's badge
is a ring cut with an even-odd fill.
